Setup — your resume

Upload the resume to analyze against (PDF, DOCX or TXT). The file is kept locally under output/uploads/; the extracted text is sent over HTTPS to the AI provider you select below (OpenRouter → DeepSeek, Z.ai GLM or OpenAI — some processing outside your country) for skill extraction and analysis. It is never sent to the extension's developer. GitHub Search receives skill-gap terms only, never resume text.

1. Collect job descriptions

Open a LinkedIn job description, then click capture. Repeat for as many JDs as you like — the list only grows.

    2. Analyze gaps

    Ranks what the collected JDs demand against your skills. The first run takes minutes (LLM judge); later runs reuse cached judgments.

    The key goes straight to the local agent, which stores it in the OS credential vault (Windows Credential Manager / macOS Keychain) — never in the browser. Uncheck "remember" to keep it for this server session only.

    Where your data goes: analysis sends your resume and job-description text to this provider over HTTPS (OpenRouter forwards to the underlying model — default DeepSeek; Z.ai GLM; OpenAI) — some processing outside your country. GitHub Search receives skill-gap terms only. Nothing goes to the extension's developer.

    Free models cost nothing, but the providers behind them may log or use your inputs for training. Job descriptions are public — your resume is not: it contains personal information (name, contact details, work history) and will be sent to these third-party providers. Your own key's paid endpoints generally do not train on inputs. Consent is required before any free-mode run.
