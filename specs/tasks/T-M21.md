## Acceptance criteria (checkable, per-change)

1. No "stays on your machine"-style claim remains anywhere user-facing
   (panel, extension README, root README). The accurate two-part truth is
   stated at the upload surface: the file is kept locally under
   `output/uploads/`; the extracted text is sent over HTTPS to the AI
   provider the user selects — never to the extension's developer.
2. Named destinations + caveat notes are visible in the panel (upload
   disclosure and near the provider select): OpenRouter forwards to the
   underlying model (e.g. DeepSeek), Z.ai GLM and OpenAI are the other
   options — some process data outside your country; GitHub Search
   receives skill-gap terms only, never resume text; free providers may
   log/use inputs for training (M16's disclaimer stays verbatim).

   versioned `disclosureConsent` flag like M16's) gates **upload and
   analyze**; `POST /api/resume` refuses with 409 unless
   `X-Disclosure-Ack: 1` rides the request (gate is not UI-only — the
   M16 lesson applied to the new touchpoint).
4. Free-mode consent text and `CONSENT_VERSION` machinery untouched —
   `m16_check` stays green.
5. Spec: §Data disclosure (M21) written; the M14 decision row's "resume
   PII never leaves the machine" claim struck as superseded; M21 decision
   rows appended; SECURITY-REVIEW.md remediation status updated (S2 fixed,
   L5 disclosure side fixed).
